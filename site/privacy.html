Privacy Policy

Effective date: EFFECTIVE_DATE_AT_PUBLISH

What we collect. If you subscribe, we collect your email address, anything you send us, and basic technical logs that our web host and email provider keep. We never collect card numbers. Tips go through outside services that have their own privacy policies.

How we use it. We use it only to run Battle Calls: to send what you signed up for, reply to you, keep the site secure, and follow the law.

We don't sell your information. We never sell, rent, or trade your information, or share it with advertisers. The only exceptions are service providers bound to keep it confidential, legal requirements, and a business transfer as described below.

Business transfer. If Battle Calls is sold, merged, or passed to a successor (including after the owner's death), subscriber information may move with the business. It goes only to someone continuing Battle Calls or a similar service, and it is never sold on its own.

Notice first. At least 30 days before a transfer, we'll email you and post a notice here. You can unsubscribe or ask us to delete your information before it moves. People who have unsubscribed are never transferred.

The new owner is bound by this policy. A new owner must honor this policy for information collected under it. Any materially different use needs your opt-in.

Changes. Material changes get a new date and an email before they take effect. They apply to information we already have only if you agree.

Your choices. Every email has an unsubscribe link, honored within 10 business days. You can also ask us to delete your information through our contact form (opens in a new tab).

Children. Battle Calls is not for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their information, and we delete it if we learn of it.

Contact: use our contact form (opens in a new tab).
