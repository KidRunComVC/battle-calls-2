Winners

Every caller who beat THE MARKET.

A Battle Call scores one public caller's own posted target against THE MARKET. The caller wins if price touches their target before the deadline. THE MARKET wins if price reaches the same distance the other way first (capped at 50% of entry). The first touch ends the bout. If nothing is touched, the deadline-day close decides it: at or past the entry in the caller's direction is a WIN BY DECISION, and a close exactly at the entry goes to the caller. DRAW and VOID are explained on the Methodology page (opens in a new tab). Every winner gets their own trophy and a spot in the hall.
