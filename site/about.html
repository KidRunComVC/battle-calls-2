About

Battle Calls: the unauthorized fight card puts opposing public market calls on a card and scores them against the chart. The call wins or loses. The person is not the subject.

On this card

  1. Round 1: Silver
  2. Round 2: Bitcoin

What unauthorized means

"Unauthorized" means the people on the card did not participate in this site and did not agree to be scored here.

What this is

  • Entertainment and education only. This is not financial, investment, or trading advice.
  • Independent commentary. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by X or anyone featured. All names and marks belong to their owners.
  • A single call says nothing about overall skill.
  • Posts are quoted under fair use and linked to the original. A later edit does not replace the locked wording. A deleted post is noted and is not republished.
  • Quotes are brief and for commentary; please read and share the original post, not a copy from this site.
  • Position disclosure: this site does not publish an editor's book, and no position is stated here. Nothing on the card is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold.
  • The price source and the timestamp are named on each bout. A locked print is not a live quote.
  • A single bout can finish by KO, by TKO when the bout defines a stop-hit, by decision, by draw, or as a no contest. The rules on that card are the rules that were locked.

What the fine print is

The fine print is an additional promotional notice. It does not replace the lines on this page, the line under the site name, or the one-line footer.

Corrections

The corrections log is newest first. No personal address is published. Name the bout, the line, and the source.
